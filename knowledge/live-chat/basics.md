# Basics of Live Chat (Live Chat 085)
**Source:** Product Foundation Courses → Live Chat / 085 Basics of Live chat (video transcript) · **Help:** search `site:sprinklr.com/help live chat widget home screen conversation screen layouts themes`

## What it is
**Live Chat** is a channel for **real-time** customer↔brand interaction. The **live chat widget** is embedded on the brand's **website** or integrated into their **mobile app**; when a customer visits, they can chat with the brand.
- **Use cases:** **digital commerce** (assist a purchase) and **customer care**.
- **Why preferred:** instant help **on the website** (no jumping to social apps); **self-serve** capability (conversational AI bot + self-serve cards); rich **UI/functional customization**.
- **Industries:** technology, retail, F&B, hospitality, government, entertainment & media, energy.

## Two components
### Home screen
- **Your Conversations** — history of past conversations on that site.
- **Self-serve cards** (client-configurable):
  - **Knowledge Base card** — nudges visitors to FAQ articles to self-resolve before chatting.
  - **Status card** — read-only info (e.g. site under maintenance, important notices).
  - **Info card** — when the brand wants the customer to interact within presented information.
  - **Guided Workflow card** — runs a predefined process (submit a support ticket, check refund/repair status, device troubleshooting); built in [[../guided-workflow/overview]] and embedded here.

### Conversation screen
- **Messages** between brand and customer.
- **Chat actions** (quick actions): delete conversation, close conversation, save transcript, start new conversation, change language.
- **Disclaimer card** — terms & conditions / important notices (e.g. "don't share credit card info").
- **Reply box** — includes:
  - **Persistent menu** — configurable steps (e.g. "restart chat" sent as a text message; a **conversational AI bot** can automate the next steps when an option is chosen).
  - **Attachment** icon (all attachment types supported), **word limit** display, **emojis**, **voice notes**, **speech-to-text**.

## Layouts (two)
- **Floating widget** — on the website or mobile app; standard live-chat use cases (support, digital commerce).
- **Full page layout** — a **shareable URL link**; use cases: share live chat via another channel, or run on **offline kiosks** in stores to capture info/feedback.

## Themes (two)
- **Classic skin** — the original UI (still used by many clients).
- **Modern skin** — newer modern design; **recommended for all clients** now.

## Notes / gaps
- Self-serve cards (esp. KB + Guided Workflow cards) are the deflection levers on the home screen — surface FAQs/workflows before a human conversation starts.
- Modern skin is the default recommendation for new deployments.
