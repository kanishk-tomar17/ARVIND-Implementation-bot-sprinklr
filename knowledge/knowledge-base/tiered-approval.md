# Tiered Approval Workflow (KB 123)
**Source:** Product Foundation Courses → Knowledge Base - Tiered Approval Workflow → **"KB Microskills - Tiered Approvals" PDF** (slide deck; no course video) · **Help:** search `site:sprinklr.com/help tiered approval workflow`

## What it is
**Tiered Approvals** is a configurable, modular approval-process engine. It **structures the approval flow** an asset must pass through before it goes live — for Knowledge Base, the relevant asset is **Articles**.

## Why it helps brands
1. **Structures, simplifies and modularises** the approval process flow so anyone can configure it.
2. **Visually represents the otherwise-invisible flow** — quick understanding of dependencies and blockers in the process.
3. **Increases efficiency via conditional logic** — automatically enables the correct approval flow(s) based on conditions (the right flow is chosen for you).

## Touchpoints / scope
- Tiered Approvals is **augmented** — it's used **in tandem with other enabled modules**, functionally touching **multiple asset types**, e.g.: **Publishing, Paid, Media Plan, Consumer Post, Article, WFM Schedule**. *(For Knowledge Base specifically, the asset type is **Articles**.)*
- Governed by the standard **governance concepts**: **Roles, Permissions, Personas, User Groups** — i.e. who can submit vs approve is driven by [[users-permissions]].

## How it maps to KB roles
- **Content Creator** submits an article → **Content Manager / Approver** approves it → published. (See the role→permission split in [[users-permissions]].)
