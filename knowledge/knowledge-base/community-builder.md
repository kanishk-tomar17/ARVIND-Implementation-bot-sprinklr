# Community Builder (KB 125)
**Source:** Product Foundation Courses → Knowledge Base - Community Builder → **"KB Microskills - Community Builder" PDF** (slide deck; no course video) · **Help:** search `site:sprinklr.com/help community builder knowledge base`

## What it is
**Community** is the **public-facing** surface where Knowledge Base **articles** are consumed by customers (a self-service web community/portal). **Community Builder** is the tool used to build/configure that community (mapping, article ordering, layout, etc.).

## Where it sits — KB touchpoints
Knowledge Base (the articles) is consumed across **multiple products**, split into:
- **Agent-facing consumption:** **Knowledge Base Builder**, **Care Console** (agents pull articles while handling cases).
- **Public-facing consumption:** **Community** (the deep-dive of this topic) and **Live Chat** (article/deflection surfaced to customers).

So KB articles flow outward to: KB Builder + Care Console (internal) and Community + Live Chat (external). Community is the **public web** endpoint.
