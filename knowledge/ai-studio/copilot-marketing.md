# Sprinklr Copilot Features for Marketing (Sprinklr AI — AI Copilots)
**Source:** sprinklr.com/help — https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/sprinklr-marketing/sprinklr-copilot-features-for-marketing/694155808057f03fa013cca1

## What it is
- AI assistant embedded in Sprinklr Marketing that helps teams create, optimise, and manage campaigns across channels at speed.
- Generates brand-aligned content and platform-tailored social posts, checks brand-guideline compliance, and surfaces sentiment, competitive trends, and performance insights.
- Automates repetitive marketing tasks and offers creative recommendations so teams focus on strategy, not manual execution.

## When to use
- You want plain-language answers about ad performance without manually digging through dashboards (Ads Reporting Copilot).
- You're building, managing, or optimising social ad campaigns and want AI assistance inline (Ads Manager Copilot).
- You need brand-compliant content/social posts generated fast across multiple platforms.
- You want real-time audience sentiment and competitive trend monitoring to drive decisions.

## Configuration steps
Two Copilot surfaces are documented for Marketing. Both are **Enabled on Request** (raise with your Sprinklr partner/account team — not self-serve).

1. **Sprinklr Copilot for Ads Reporting** — chat-based assistant that turns complex ad data into instant insights; query the dashboard and summarise key ad parameters.
   - Enablement: Enabled on Request
   - Access: Paid > Ads Reporting > **Ads Dashboard Copilot**
   - RBAC support: Yes
   - AI+ Studio integration: No
2. **Sprinklr Copilot for Ads Manager** — assists with creating, managing, and optimising social advertising campaigns.
   - Enablement: Enabled on Request
   - Access: Paid > Ads Manager > **Ads Manager CoPilot**
   - RBAC support: Yes
   - AI+ Studio integration: No
