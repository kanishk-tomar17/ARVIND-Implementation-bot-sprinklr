# Sprinklr Copilot Features for Insights (Sprinklr AI — AI Copilots)
**Source:** sprinklr.com/help — https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/sprinklr-insights/sprinklr-copilot-features-for-insights/6941563af0afa271d135fb97

## What it is
- AI Copilot built into Sprinklr Insights that analyzes customer and market data.
- Surfaces emerging trends, brand-health signals, and audience sentiment in real time across social, digital, and customer interactions.
- Automates data interpretation: summarizes reports, flags anomalies, gives predictive insights, and recommends strategic actions.

## When to use
- You want a fast natural-language summary of a report or dashboard instead of reading every widget.
- You need anomaly detection / "what changed" on brand health or sentiment.
- You're researching a topic before building Listening queries (use the Research copilot on the Launch Pad).
- You want AI help building a survey or a benchmarking analysis.

## Configuration steps
This article is a capability/availability matrix, not a click-by-click guide. Enablement is per-feature; most require a request to Sprinklr or AI+ Studio. By feature:

1. **Copilot for Benchmarking Dashboards**
   - Enablement: enabled on request (raise with Sprinklr).
   - Access: Role-Based Access Control available.
   - Location: Benchmarking > Benchmarking Assistant > Benchmarking Assistant.
   - AI+ Studio integration: No.
2. **Copilot for Building Surveys**
   - Enablement: enabled on request.
   - Location: Survey Builder.
   - AI+ Studio integration: No.
3. **Copilot for Research (Social Listening)**
   - Enablement: enabled with AI+ Studio.
   - Location: Launch Pad.
4. **Copilot for Listening Dashboard (Social Listening)**
   - Enablement: enabled with AI+ Studio.
   - Location: Listening Dashboard.

General path to turn on: confirm the feature is enabled (request via Sprinklr / set up AI+ Studio where required), then grant the relevant roles via RBAC, then access from the module location above.

## Notes & gaps
