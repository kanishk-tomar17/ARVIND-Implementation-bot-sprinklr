# Sprinklr Copilot Features for Social (Sprinklr AI — AI Copilots)
**Source:** sprinklr.com/help — https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/sprinklr-social/sprinklr-copilot-features-for-social/694156172727657ea2ffba31

## What it is
- AI assistant inside Sprinklr Social for Social Media Managers and Reporting Analysts.
- Natural-language helper for publishing, engagement, and monitoring across 20+ channels.
- Two distinct Copilot surfaces: one for Planning/Publishing, one for Social Reporting.

## When to use
- Drafting on-brand, platform-specific posts and getting best-time-to-post recommendations.
- Scheduling and amplifying content across many channels from one place.
- Pulling fast insights on trends, audience sentiment, and competitor activity.
- Automating repetitive work — tagging, scheduling, reporting — to free up strategy time.
- Building or interrogating social dashboards in plain language instead of manual config.

## Configuration steps
1. **Request enablement.** Both Copilot features are **Enabled on Request** — raise the request with your Sprinklr partner/CSM to turn them on for the environment.
2. **Confirm RBAC.** Both features respect **role-based access control**; ensure target users have the right role/permissions to see and use Copilot.
3. **Access the Publishing Copilot:**
   - Go to **Publishing > Calendar > Social Copilot**.
   - Use it to create, schedule, and amplify content; check compliance; get posting-time suggestions.
4. **Access the Reporting Copilot:**
   - Go to **Reporting > Dashboards > Social Dashboard Copilot**.
   - Use it to analyze trends, sentiment, and competitor activity and surface insights.

Feature reference:
- **Sprinklr Copilot for Planning in Social** — Publishing > Calendar > Social Copilot; Enabled on Request; RBAC: Yes; not available in AI+ Studio.
- **Sprinklr Copilot for Social Reporting** — Reporting > Dashboards > Social Dashboard Copilot; Enabled on Request; RBAC: Yes; not available in AI+ Studio.
