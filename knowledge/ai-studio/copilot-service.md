# Sprinklr Copilot Features for Service (Sprinklr AI — AI Copilots)
**Source:** sprinklr.com/help — https://www.sprinklr.com/help/articles/sprinklr-service/sprinklr-copilot-features-for-service/694156088057f03fa0140969

## What it is
- A suite of AI-powered Copilot features for Sprinklr Service that speed up and personalise customer support across digital channels.
- Covers three areas: agent assistance (Agent Copilot), quality management (Copilot in Automated QM), and reporting (Service Analytics Copilot).
- Handles conversation summarisation, response suggestions, auto-categorisation/prioritisation of tickets, and real-time sentiment/intent insights.

## When to use
- Agents need real-time help resolving cases faster (summaries, suggested replies) — use **Agent Copilot** in Care Console.
- QA teams want to evaluate 100% of cases instead of random sampling — use **Copilot in Automated QM (AQM)** with AI-generated checklists.
- Managers need custom service reports without manual dashboard building — use **Service Analytics Copilot**.

## Configuration steps
All three features are **Enabled on Request** — the org must have them activated by Sprinklr before they appear.

1. **Agent Assist / Agent Copilot**
   - Surfaces on the **Care Console** for real-time case resolution.
   - Enabled on request.
   - Access is controlled by **record page access** permissions (granted at Care Console manager level).

2. **Quality Management — Sprinklr Copilot for Admins in Automated QM (AQM)**
   - Uses AI-generated checklists to score service parameters; auto-evaluates all cases (no random sampling).
   - Configure via **AI+ Studio > Manage Copilots**.
   - Appears in **Audit Checklist > Record Form**.

3. **Reporting — Service Analytics Copilot**
   - Generates custom reports with filters and metrics for performance analysis and actionable insights.
   - Find at **Reporting > Dashboards > Service Analytics Copilot**.
   - Also available in **AI+ Studio**.
