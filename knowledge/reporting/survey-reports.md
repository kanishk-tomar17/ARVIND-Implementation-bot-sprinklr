# Survey Reports (Reporting 072)
**Source:** Product Foundation Courses → Reporting / 072 Survey Reports (video transcript + Survey Reporting dashboard screenshot) · **Help:** search `site:sprinklr.com/help survey reporting CSAT NPS submit rate bounce rate root cause`

## What it is
Reporting on surveys built in the **Survey Builder** (questions, ratings, CSAT/NPS). After building/sending a survey, you report on responses. The dashboard typically has **Survey Summary** and **Root Cause Analysis** tabs.

## Survey Summary use cases (key widgets)
- **Surveys Sent** — count + trend across dates, aggregated by **channel** (Live Chat, Community, Twitter, Facebook, Email, Instagram, TikTok, Automatic Alerts).
- **Surveys Failed** — failed-survey count + trend by channel/date.
- **Surveys Clicked** — clicked but not necessarily filled (e.g. 53 clicked) by channel/date.
- **Surveys Submitted** — submitted out of those sent, trend by date.
- **Submit Rate** = surveys submitted ÷ surveys sent (e.g. ~4.28%).
- **Bounce Rate** = (clicked − submitted) ÷ surveys sent — can be negative if submitted > clicked.
- **Feedback Card Rating** — case rating per case (shows case + rating).
- **Agent-wise survey ratings** — which agent on which case got what rating (e.g. "How satisfied are you with the agent?" Extremely Satisfied/Dissatisfied) → supervisor can act.

## Root Cause Analysis
- Per **case ID + agent**: the survey questions and answers, pinpointing how agents are performing (e.g. "Was there a delay solving the problem?" Yes/No).
- **Supervisor views:** case severity (critical/high/low), whether the problem was common/recurring, and a **Supervisor Remark** field (case ID, agent, brand/fan message counts, remark after auditing the case) — to coach agents.
