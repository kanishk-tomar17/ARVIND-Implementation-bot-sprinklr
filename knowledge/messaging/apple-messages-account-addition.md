# Apple Messages for Business Account Addition (Messaging 055)
**Source:** Product Foundation Courses → Messaging / 055 Apple Messages for Business Account Addition (video transcript + "Add Apple Messages for Business account" screen screenshot) · **Help:** search `site:sprinklr.com/help Apple Messages for Business account register tester approval`

## What it is
**Apple Messages for Business** lets brands engage with customers on Apple devices — enables **reactive messaging** + conversational AI on Sprinklr.

## Eligibility
- An **asynchronous messaging platform staffed by live agents** during business hours.
- Use of an **Apple-approved MSP** like Sprinklr.

## High-level steps
1. Create an **Apple Business Register** account (brand logs in with its Apple ID).
2. Create an **internal test account** in Apple Business Register.
3. Configure it, connect to Sprinklr, test, then get Apple approval for **public launch**.

## Create the internal test account (Apple Business Register)
1. Fill/verify the **organizational information** when prompted.
2. Select business account type: click **Advanced** → **Internal test account** → give it a name → Next.
3. Select the **technical contact** → Next to configure the **Messaging Service Provider**.
4. On the MSP screen, select **Sprinklr** → Next.
5. Add **tester Apple IDs** (can add more later); optionally send email instructions → Next. *Only authorized testers can test the account.*
6. To connect: scroll to **Messaging Service Provider configuration** → **Test your Messaging Service Provider connection** → **Connect** → a new window opens → **copy the link** and save it (used to connect the account to Sprinklr).

## Add the account in Sprinklr
1. Open a new page → search **Accounts** → **Own Social Accounts** → **Add Account**.
2. Search **Apple Messages for Business**.
3. Enter a **Display Name** (shown in Sprinklr) and paste the **URL** copied from the Apple Business Register process.
4. **Save** → account added.

## Add tester Apple IDs (in Sprinklr)
- Under **Messages for Business Accounts** → select the account → scroll to **Testers Apple IDs** → **Edit** → add Apple IDs → **Add**.
- Optionally **send testing instructions** (email contains the link that opens the Messages app) → **Send Now** → **Submit**.
- Scroll to **Links** → copy the **conversational link** → open in a new tab → redirects to the **Messages app** → converse with the internal test account.

## Tester troubleshooting
- The tester's Apple ID must match the one **registered on their iOS device**.
- **Whitelist** the tester IDs: Settings → Messages → ensure **iMessage** toggle is ON (default), and **Send & Receive** includes the **Apple ID** (by default only the SIM phone number is present). Whitelisting is based on the Apple email ID — important step.

## Approval process (public launch)
1. Configure an **end-to-end user flow** in the internal test account per Apple's guidelines.
2. Create a **screen recording** of the configured user experience.
3. Share with the **Sprinklr messaging product team** for review/feedback.
4. After product review, **submit to Apple**. The video must accurately represent the user journey, use Apple's features efficiently, and show the best UX (follow template best practices — see [[apple-messages-templates]]).
5. After Apple approves, the account is ready for **public launch**.
