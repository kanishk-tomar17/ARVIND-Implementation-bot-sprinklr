# Messaging Channels Overview (Messaging 052)
**Source:** Product Foundation Courses → Messaging / 052 Messaging Channels Overview (video transcript + "Messaging Channels on Sprinklr" slide screenshot) · **Help:** search `site:sprinklr.com/help messaging channels supported`

## Messaging vs Social channels
- **Messaging channels** (WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, SMS, etc.) — **private, one-to-one** communication; used for personalised customer support interactions.
- **Social channels** (Facebook, Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn) — **public** engagement to reach a wider audience; broadcast updates, news, content to a community.

## Messaging channels supported on Sprinklr
Per the slide:
- WhatsApp Business
- Facebook Messenger
- Apple Messages for Business
- Google Business Messaging
- Twitter DM
- WeChat
- Instagram DM
- Line
- Viber
- **Source Agnostic** (channel-agnostic option)

All can be used to reach customers via the various Sprinklr modules.

## Messaging modules — proactive vs reactive
Messaging modules facilitate both **proactive** (brand initiates) and **reactive** (brand responds) engagement.

**Proactive outreach** (brand starts the conversation):
- **Marketing Campaigns** — configure the end-to-end marketing lifecycle across messaging channels; audience targeting → execution → reporting across all channels.
- **Journey Facilitator** — visualise the customer journey flow; plan end-to-end journeys (targeting, interactions, performance analysis). See [[journey-builder-basics]].

**Reactive engagement** (brand responds to a customer query):
- **Agent workflows** — streamline via the **Care Console**, an omni-channel console for agents to handle interactions with timely, consistent responses.
- **Conversational AI** — chatbot flows that preemptively understand and mitigate customer queries, enhancing the experience.
