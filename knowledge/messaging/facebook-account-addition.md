# Facebook Account Addition (Messaging 054)
**Source:** Product Foundation Courses → Messaging / 054 Facebook Account Addition (video transcript + Add Account channel-picker screenshot) · **Help:** search `site:sprinklr.com/help add Facebook business manager page own social accounts`

## What it is
Adding Facebook to Sprinklr covers two things: the **Facebook Business Manager** (manage ad accounts, pages, and people) and individual **Facebook Pages** (where brands connect with fans/customers). Any Facebook page is treated as a **company page** within Sprinklr.

## Add a Facebook Business Manager
- Lets you manage ad accounts, pages, and people with access — all in Sprinklr.
- **Requirements:** the user adding it must be an **admin** of the account natively; **paid media advertising must be enabled** in your environment (contact your Success Manager to enable).
- **Steps:**
  1. Open a new page → search **Accounts** → **Own Social Accounts** (under Sprinklr Social) → **Add Account**.
  2. Search **Facebook Business Manager** → redirected to **Login with Facebook**.
  3. Continue with your account and grant the necessary **permissions**.
  4. Redirected back to Sprinklr → select the Business Manager to add → configure **account settings** → **Save**.

## Add a Facebook Page
- **Pre-step:** log out of all Facebook sessions across all devices.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open a new page → search **Accounts** → **Own Social Accounts** → **Add Account**.
  2. Search **Facebook Page**. *Your account must have a page role of **admin, editor, or moderator**.*
  3. Optionally **enable/disable private messages** → **Add**.
  4. Redirected to the **login window** → grant access → **Continue**.
  5. Back in Sprinklr → select the **page** to add → **Add**.
  6. Optionally configure **account settings** → **Save** → account added.
