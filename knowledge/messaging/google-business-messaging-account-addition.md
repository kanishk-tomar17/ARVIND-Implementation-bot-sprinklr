# Google Business Messaging Account Addition (Messaging 056)
**Source:** Product Foundation Courses → Messaging / 056 Google Business Messaging Account Addition (video transcript + "Account Addition Flow" 4-step slide screenshot) · **Help:** search `site:sprinklr.com/help Google Business Messaging account business ID entry points`

## What it is
**Google Business Messaging (GBM)** lets businesses connect with customers via entry points like **Google Maps** and **Google Search** — customers initiate conversations directly from search results / business listings. All Google My Business users can add messaging to their listing (shows in both Search and Maps).
> Each customer **must be approved by Google** when adding an account to Sprinklr — reach out to your Success Manager.

## Entry points
- **Local** (rely on locations) — Google Maps, location search, place sheets. → require **Place IDs**.
- **Non-local** (don't rely on locations) — Google Search, site links, phone numbers. → require **phone numbers + contact URLs**.
- Both can be incorporated into your user flow.

## Account addition flow (4 steps)
1. **Initiation** — brand provides required details for GBM account config; **Sprinklr product team** adds the account in the **GBM Console** and shares a **Business ID**; brand adds the account in Sprinklr using that Business ID.
2. **Setup** — set up the account in Sprinklr: implement available features, configure the user flow, and run **UAT**.
3. **Verification** — after UAT sign-off from the brand, the product team raises a request to Google to verify; the brand verifies the account via a **mail sent by Google**.
4. **Launch** — all features are made live **gradually**.

## Requirements (share with product team before initiation)
- **GBM Console (mandatory):** **Agent name** (visible to end user); chosen **entry points** (local → Place IDs; non-local → phone numbers + contact URLs); a brand-specific **privacy URL**.
- **Add in Sprinklr:** the **Business ID** (provided by the Sprinklr product team).
- **Verification step:** account **owner name**, owner **email address**, and a **website URL**.

## Add the account in Sprinklr
1. Open a new page → search **Accounts** → **Own Social Accounts** → **Add Account**.
2. Search **Google Business Messaging**.
3. Enter a **Display Name** and the **Business ID** (from the product team).
4. **Save** → account added → proceed to the account **setup** step.
