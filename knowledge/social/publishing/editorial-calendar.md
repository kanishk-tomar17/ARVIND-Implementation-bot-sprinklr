# Editorial Calendar (Sprinklr Social — Publishing)
**Source:** sprinklr.com/help — Publishing sub-area (multiple articles; see links below)

## What it is
- A tool to plan, organize, and schedule content across social, non-social, and paid channels.
- Lets teams build a content plan for multiple channels/platforms and collaborate so everyone stays aligned.
- Supports campaign management (organic and paid) alongside content, across multiple channels and geographies in unified views.
- Tracks performance to support data-driven content decisions. Related: [[web-analytics]], [[ai-in-publishing]].
- Reached via Sprinklr Social → Publish → Editorial Calendar.

## Key features & how to use

### Views, visualization & personalization
- Gantt and Timeline views: Year, Quarter, Month, Week.
- Calendar and Timeline views: Month, Week, Day, List.
- Color-tag content for categorization.
- Customizable content previews showing: name, image, channel type, account details.
- Personalized Editorial Calendar Boards built from filtered and grouped combinations.
- Bulk capabilities: bulk import, export, and bulk actions.
- Collaboration: share with team members and external stakeholders; involve org stakeholders in campaign planning.

### Work Week view
- Purpose: show the Editorial Calendar for work days only instead of the full week. You choose which days are included.
- Enable:
  1. Go to Sprinklr Social → Editorial Calendar (within Publish).
  2. In the Editorial Calendar window, open the **Options** icon menu.
  3. Select **View Options**.
  4. Toggle on **Show Work Week View**.
- Customize which days appear:
  1. Click the **Edit Work Week** icon.
  2. In the **Work Week Setting** popup, select/deselect the days you want.
  3. Click **Save**.
- Default: 5-day work week, Monday–Friday.
- Post cards display according to the configured work week calendar.
- Prerequisite: this capability must be enabled by your Success Manager.

### Revamped navigation (left menu bar)
- The board/calendar selector moved from a dropdown into a **new left-side menu bar** for easier navigation between boards and calendars.
- Six primary navigation options in the left panel:
  - **Favorite Boards** — quick access to boards marked as favorites.
  - **Workspace Editorial Calendar** — space to plan and manage content within a workspace.
  - **Global Editorial Calendar** — all editorial content across the entire organization.
  - **My Boards** — personal boards for content specific to your needs.
  - **Standard Boards** — regular boards for general content planning/management.
  - **Shared Boards** — collaborative boards accessible by multiple users.
- Aimed at the Social Planning & Publishing Persona App to streamline content and marketing planning. Related: [[approval-workflows]].
