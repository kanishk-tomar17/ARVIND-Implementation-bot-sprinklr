# SlideShare (Sprinklr Social — Channel)
**Source:** sprinklr.com/help — SlideShare channel (multiple articles; see links below)

## What it is
- SlideShare is a content-hosting platform for professional presentations, documents, and infographics. Sprinklr lets you publish presentations to a connected SlideShare account from the Social publishing tool.
- Core capability: upload or select a document, add a title/description/tags, set a privacy level, and publish immediately, save as draft, or schedule for later.
- Privacy and access controls (downloads, embeds, contact sharing, secret URLs) are set per post at publish time.
- This is a publishing-only channel in the help content — engagement and reporting are not documented for SlideShare specifically (see [[publishing]]).

## Key features & how to use

### Publishing — Create and post a presentation
Open the composer and select the account:
- Click the **Publish** icon in the top navigation bar, then choose **Create Post**.
- In the **Create Post** window, under **Select Accounts**, pick your SlideShare account(s) (shown as e.g. "Acme Co | Slide Share"). Use **Advanced Search** (top-right of the account field) to filter accounts.
- The post type tab shows **Presentation** once a SlideShare account is selected.

Add the content (the composer offers two buttons on the left, **Select Document** and **Upload Document**):
- **Upload Document** to upload a new file, or **Select Document** to pull existing content. The article notes you can source from system, Favourites, SAM (Sprinklr Asset Manager), or a URL.
- **Enter Title** in the title field and fill the **Description** box.

Configure metadata and privacy:
- **Tags** — add tags from the **Add Tags** dropdown.
- **Privacy** dropdown — choose the visibility level:
  - **Public** — "Discoverable by anyone on or off SlideShare." Exposes an **Allow Downloads** checkbox.
  - **Limited** — "Discoverable by users with the link to the post." Adds options: **Allow Downloads**, **Share with Contacts**, **Generate Secret URL**, and **Allow Embeds**.
- **Campaign** (required field, marked with *) — select a campaign from the **Select a Campaign** dropdown; tick **Set as Default** to reuse it. See [[campaigns]].
- **URL Shortener** — pick a shortener from **Select a URL Shortener** to abbreviate links.
- **Properties** — apply post properties via the **Hide/Show Properties** expander (e.g. Naming Convention, "Add Text to Name"). The screenshot showed 57 configurable properties; these are environment-specific custom fields.
- **Approval** — set an **Approval Type** and add an optional note before submitting.

Preview, publish, or schedule:
- Use the **Preview** icon (bottom-left) to review. Note: "Mobile preview is not available for SlideShare" — use **View Web preview** instead.
- **Submit for Approval** / **Post** — publish immediately (label depends on whether approval is required).
- **Save as Draft** — store without publishing.
- **Schedule Post** — schedule future publication; select month, date, and time, with repeat options.
- **Publish Another** — checkbox to keep composing additional posts.

## Common issues & fixes
- **No mobile preview** — SlideShare posts cannot be previewed in mobile view in the composer; this is expected. Use **View Web preview** to check layout.
- **Campaign is mandatory** — the Campaign field is required; the post cannot be submitted without selecting one.

## Notes & gaps
- **Prerequisites:** a SlideShare account must be connected to the Sprinklr environment before it appears under Select Accounts. Account connection/authentication steps are not covered in this article.
- **Permissions:** publishing permissions and approval-path configuration are governed by your environment's roles and approval workflow ([[approvals]], [[rule-engine]]) — not detailed here.
- **Properties** vary by environment (custom fields); the "57 properties" count is illustrative of one tenant, not a fixed list.
- **Engagement & reporting** for SlideShare are not documented in the help center; treat this as a publish-only channel unless your environment indicates otherwise. See [[engagement-dashboards]] and [[reporting]] for general Social analytics.
