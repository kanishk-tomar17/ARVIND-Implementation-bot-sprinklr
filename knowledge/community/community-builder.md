# Community Builder (Community 180)
**Source:** Product Foundation Courses → Community / 180 Community Builder (video transcript + demo) · **Help:** search `site:sprinklr.com/help community builder category hierarchy`

## What it is
A **Sprinklr Community** is a support tool / forum where a group of people with common interests **discuss best practices, advice, troubleshooting questions**, etc. Brands use communities for **customer feedback, brand recognition, testing product ideas, and mobilizing customer loyalty**.

## Create a community
- **Launchpad → search "Community Builder"** → search existing communities or click **Create**.
- Form fields: **Organization name**, **Community name**, **Base language**, **Secondary languages** — and add the base + secondary languages to the **Active Languages** section. **Save**.

## Per-community management (⋯ menu)
Options include: **Edit**, **Category Hierarchy Management**, **Roles and Permissions**, **Badge Management**, **Rank Management**, **Email Templates**.

### Category Hierarchy Management
- Communities have **Categories**; **Topics** live **inside a category** (a topic is always created within a category — topics are the discussion forums).
- **Create Hierarchy → Create Category:** set **name, description, image, order** (display order in the community), **desktop/mobile images**, and **map Knowledge Base folders**.
  - **To show KB articles in the community:** first create the articles in **Knowledge Base** inside a **folder**, **map that folder** here in category mapping, then go back to KB and **publish** the articles so they appear in the community.
- **Translations:** after saving a category, pick a language (from active community languages) → add the translated category name/content → save. Switching the community language then shows the translation.

### Roles & Permissions
- Create a **role**, grant permissions, then **add the profiles / profile list** the permissions apply to → Save. (Community-specific; see [[global-workspace-roles]].)

### Gamification — Badge & Rank Management
- The community offers **gamification**: users are allotted a **badge** or **rank**; by rank they appear on a **leaderboard**.
